Research & Publications

Adversarial Perturbations for Evaluating Reasoning in NLP Models

Presented at UNT’s Research Day Conference.

This research focuses on stress-testing Large Language Models (LLMs) using adversarial perturbations to evaluate their reasoning capabilities. By introducing subtle noise and distractor statements, we analyze the robustness of Chain-of-Thought (CoT) prompting and identify failure modes in current state-of-the-art models.

Ongoing Research:

"MCQ Distractor Ranking for Evaluating Multi-Step Reasoning in NLP Models" — Developing a novel framework to rank distractor quality in multiple-choice questions to better assess LLM logical consistency.
